Three nights

Each night has one job. Nobody is expected to attend all three, but most people do, mainly out of guilt.

01

Thu 18 · from 19:00

The Lighting

Twelve hundred lanterns go up along the harbour wall in forty minutes. The trick is that they are lit in sequence, so the whole street turns on like something enormous waking up.

02

Fri 19 · from 18:30

The Parade

Anyone can walk in it. There is no rehearsal, no costume requirement and no route map — the route is simply wherever the front of the parade decides to go, which is a known and accepted risk.

03

Sat 20 · from 21:00

The Send-off

The market closes, the crowd goes quiet, and one firework — genuinely one, a very good one — goes up over the water. Then everybody goes home and the street is spotless by seven.
