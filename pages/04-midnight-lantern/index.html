Four hours, four errands

00:00

The shutters go up

Nothing is on the shelves in daylight. Everything is brought out at midnight and put away again at four, which is why you have never found us open.

01:20

The paper is cut

Six sheets, one bamboo frame, one candle well. If the paper tears we keep it and sell it cheaper, with the tear shown in the photograph.

02:45

The first test burn

Every lantern is lit once here, on the counter, and watched for eleven minutes. If the light wobbles it does not leave the shop.

03:58

Someone walks home

The last thing that happens each night: one lantern goes out of the door in somebody's hand, down the street, and around the corner.
