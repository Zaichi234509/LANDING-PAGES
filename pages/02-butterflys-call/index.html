01 — Field notes

Field notes

They do not lead.

A spirit butterfly will not show you the way. It waits at the edge of your vision until you have already chosen a direction, then goes ahead of you as if it had always been going there.

Papilio comitans

They travel at dusk.

Never at noon, never at true dark. The flock rises in the twenty minutes when the sky is two colours at once, and settles again before the first star is certain.

Vespertina rubra

They answer a name.

Not yours. Someone's. Field members are asked to say it out loud only once per season, and to write down what happens in the twelve seconds afterwards.

Vocatus animae
Stop chasing.
They arrive the moment you rest.
