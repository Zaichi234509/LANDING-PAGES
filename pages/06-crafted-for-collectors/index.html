We release four objects a year. Each is made by one person, in one workshop, in a number of hours we are happy to state out loud. Nothing is restocked.

The atelier

The atelier

Speed is the only luxury we have refused. Every object below could be made in a tenth of the time by a process we already own, and we have chosen not to install it, which is the entire and only reason this page exists.

A collector does not buy an object. A collector accepts responsibility for it — for keeping it, repairing it, and eventually passing it to somebody who will do the same. We make things worth that trouble.

I

The Censer

Cast in bronze from a single wax model, then destroyed — the mould, not the model. The lid seats with a quarter turn and holds for a lifetime without a gasket, which took the maker four attempts and nine months to achieve.

Maker
Zhao, foundry floor two
Material
Cast bronze, hand-patinated
Hours
164
Edition
Twelve
$2,400

II

The Folding Fan

Twenty-two ribs of aged bamboo, split from one culm so the grain runs continuously across the open fan. The paper is painted flat, then folded — which means every blossom is placed knowing exactly where the crease will fall.

Maker
Lin, paper studio
Material
Bamboo, mulberry paper, pigment
Hours
96
Edition
Thirty
$1,150

III

The Lacquer Box

Thirty-one coats of urushi, each cured for a week in a damp cabinet before the next is applied. Seven months of the build time is simply waiting. The inlay is crushed shell, laid one fragment at a time under magnification.

Maker
Ito, lacquer room
Material
Urushi over paulownia, shell inlay
Hours
412 across 9 months
Edition
Six
$3,800

IV

The Silk Panel

Hand-embroidered on double-ply silk stretched on a standing frame. Two people work it from opposite sides so the reverse is as finished as the front — the panel is intended to hang in a room, not against a wall.

Makers
Wen & Qi, frame three
Material
Mulberry silk, silk floss, gold thread
Hours
1,140 combined
Edition
Two per year
$6,200

Acquisition is by appointment

There is no cart, no checkout and no express shipping. You will be offered a time, a chair and the object itself, and you may take as long as you like. Most people take about an hour.
