Our little adventure

It was not a big adventure. We went four stops on the tram, got off at the wrong one on purpose, and were home by dark. It has been the best day of the year twice now.

page one

It started with a bad idea

“Let's get on the tram and get off when it looks interesting,” she said, which is not a plan and everybody involved knew it was not a plan.

We got on. It was 11:04. There were four stops before it looked interesting and by then we had missed lunch.

page two

The wrong stop was excellent

A street with three shops on it, all closed, and one bakery that was open and had exactly two things left. We bought both. One was mystery.

The mystery one turned out to be red bean. She had known. She had absolutely known.

page three

We found a very good hill

Twenty minutes up, and at the top a bench with somebody's name carved into it and a view of absolutely nothing remarkable. We stayed for an hour.

She said the view being unremarkable was the entire reason it was good. Nobody was there to compete with us for it.

page four

Then it rained, obviously

Not gently. We got under an awning with a man selling umbrellas who found the situation extremely funny and refused, on principle, to sell us one.

Eleven minutes. Then sun, immediately, like nothing had happened. She said the weather was "showing off".

page five · the end

Home by dark, and that was it

Four tram stops, one bakery, one hill, eleven minutes of rain. Nothing happened. Nobody learned anything. It cost about nine mora.

“Best kind of day,” she said at the door. “You can't summarise it, so nobody can take it off you.”
