The colour that shouts

Fired four times over copper at 820°C. This is the loudest the pigment gets and the only chapter where we let it be cheerful. Small pieces only — a cuff, a pin, a pair of studs. Any larger and it stops being elegant.

The colour that needs a window

Gold ruby glass: the red comes from colloidal gold suspended in the melt, which means the object is genuinely worth something before it is even blown. Unlit it is nearly black. Held to a window it is the best thing in the house.
